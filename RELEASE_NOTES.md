## Glass Tube 0.4.0

New
- Channel pages: banner, avatar, subscribers, description, Subscribe button, and Videos / Shorts / Live / Playlists tabs. Open them by tapping the channel under the player or an avatar in the lists
- Subscriptions without a Google account (kept on the phone): "Subscriptions" on the home page shows their newest videos, and they're listed on the You page. Signed in, subscribing also subscribes on YouTube
- Subtitles: choose a language in the player menu (it's remembered)
- Picture-in-picture: leaving the app during a video keeps it playing in a small window (can be turned off)
- SponsorBlock: sponsored and other unwanted parts are skipped automatically and shown in colour on the seek bar; categories can be chosen in the settings
- Warning on the home page when the Google sign-in has expired, with a button to sign in again
- Downloads for offline watching (choose the quality), in the "Downloads" section of the You page
- Queue ("Play next") and your own playlists: long-press any video for Play next / Save to playlist / Download / Share / Go to channel
- Search filters: videos, channels or playlists
- Chapters on the seek bar, and a preview picture with the chapter name while scrubbing
- Fullscreen: swipe up and down on the left side for brightness, on the right side for volume

Improved
- The player shrinks into the mini player after a short pull down (no need to drag it all the way)
- Closing the mini player with X has the same disappearing animation as swiping it away
- The active tab has a white glow instead of the blue colour
