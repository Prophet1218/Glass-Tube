## Glass Tube 0.2.0

New
- Mini player: closing the player shrinks the video into a glass mini player above the tab bar (with play/pause and close). Tap it or swipe it up to open the full player again; drag the video down to shrink it
- The tab bar shrinks into a small glass circle on the left while you scroll down, and comes back when you scroll up. The search button stays on the right, and the mini player moves into the gap between them
- Liquid glass tab capsule like on iOS: the active tab sits on a frosted capsule; pressing makes it grow into a clear liquid glass capsule that you can drag across the tabs
- Animation when switching between tabs
- Player buttons are now real liquid glass: the video bends at their edges
- New seek bar: light blue fading to white, with a comet-like glow behind the head; current time on the left, total time on the right
- Slimmer search bar and tab bar

Changed
- The player now lives inside the main screen (needed for the mini player). YouTube links from other apps open there too
