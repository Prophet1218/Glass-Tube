## Glass Tube 0.3.0

New
- Comments: open them from the card under the description (they slide up under the video), also on Shorts
- Like and dislike buttons (signed in with Google)
- Swipe up on the video to go fullscreen (the phone turns), swipe down to come back
- Swipe the mini player down to close it, with a sink-and-fade animation
- Play/pause morphs between the two shapes, like in the YouTube app
- The player's settings menu is liquid glass over the video too, with a loop option
- Shorts redesigned like YouTube Shorts (channel and title at the bottom, like / comments / share on the right) and they start much faster (the next ones are loaded ahead, smaller start buffer)
- New settings, grouped into animated sections: playback (quality on Wi-Fi and mobile data, default speed, skip length, autoplay, background playback, resume where you left off), Shorts (loop), appearance (theme, shrinking tab bar, reduced transparency), history and privacy, account
- The quality selector in the settings is a sliding glass switch

Improved
- Mini player animation: arced path, slows down gently at both ends; the player buttons pop in when it's fully open
- No loading ring inside the play button any more
- The play button isn't cut off when pressed
- The tab capsule no longer jumps when you press and release it

Removed
- HDR option
